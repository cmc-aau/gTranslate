Models

gTranslate predicts translation tables using an ensemble of five classifiers. The models used by detect_table are provided as a separate download (see gTranslate Models).

Classifiers

The ensemble was selected by comparing seven classifiers and all combinations of three and five classifiers in the gTranslate manuscript (see Validation). It consists of:

Classifier

Model file

Hyperparameters

AdaBoost

ada_multi_class.pkl.gz

50 estimators of decision trees with a maximum depth of 3, learning rate of 1

Decision tree

dt_multi_class.pkl.gz

Gini criterion, balanced class weights

k-nearest neighbours

knn_multi_class.pkl.gz

2 neighbours, Manhattan distance, uniform weights

Multi-layer perceptron

mlp_multi_class.pkl.gz

Hidden layers of 20 and 10 neurons, tanh activation, Adam solver, alpha of 0.0001

XGBoost

xgb_multi_class.pkl.gz

50 estimators, learning rate of 0.1, subsample of 0.5

Hyperparameter values were selected by grid search with 5-fold cross-validation. Features are scaled to between 0 and 1 (min-max scaling) for the k-nearest neighbours and multi-layer perceptron classifiers. The classifiers are implemented with scikit-learn and XGBoost, and the file label_encoder.pkl.gz maps the classifier outputs to translation tables.

Training data

The models distributed with gTranslate 0.0.4 and later are the models evaluated in the gTranslate manuscript (see Validation). They were trained on genomes from GTDB release R09-RS220, dereplicated to at most 100 genomes per species to avoid biasing the models towards species with many sequenced genomes. This gave 356,020 genomes, of which 354,299 (99.52%) use table 11, 1,521 (0.43%) use table 4, and 200 (0.06%) use table 25.

As the GTDB is updated, the models can be retrained to include new genomes and taxa, including newly identified reassignments of the UGA stop codon.

Ground truth

The expected translation table of each training genome is assigned from its taxonomic classification by the ground_truth command:

  • Table 25: GTDB orders Absconditabacterales and BD1-5 (class JAEDAM01).

  • Table 4:

    • GTDB order Mycoplasmatales, species Zinderia insecticola, and Eggerthellaceae genera Equivita and Gorillivita.

    • GTDB Minisyncoccia family JAKLIH01 and four species of the genus GCA-2747955, in which a UGA reassignment was identified in the gTranslate manuscript.

    • NCBI species Ca. Hodgkinia cicadicola, Ca. Nasuia deltocephalinicola, Ca. Stammera capleta, Ca. Organicella extenuata and Ca. Pinguicoccus supinus, which are not in the GTDB as their highly reduced genomes cannot be robustly placed in the GTDB reference trees.

  • Unresolved: GTDB species Providencia_A siddallii and Providencia_A siddallii_A, whose translation table is unclear (Manzano-Marín et al., 2023). These genomes are excluded from training.

  • Table 11: all other genomes.

The Minisyncoccia and Providencia_A rules were added after the analyses in the gTranslate manuscript, so the ground truth produced by the ground_truth command differs slightly from the one used to train the distributed models. The ground truth for individual genomes can be set with the --manual_gt_file argument.

Training your own models

The models can be retrained, for example to include genomes from a newer GTDB release:

  1. ground_truth: assign the expected translation table to each genome from its taxonomy.

  2. build_features: call genes and calculate the features for each genome.

  3. fit_models: train the five classifiers using the features and ground truth.

The resulting directory can be used with detect_table through the --custom_model_path argument or the GTRANSLATE_MODEL_PATH environment variable.
